# ECOCLEAN SL Data Governance Policy

ECOCLEAN SL is the operational data controller. Access follows least privilege and the existing Citizen, Staff, Supervisor, Administrator and National Administrator hierarchy. Evidence access is limited by ownership, assignment and jurisdiction; administrative access is audited.

Production operators must configure documented retention periods for accounts, reports, task evidence, support conversations, notifications, sessions, audit logs, security events and backups. Legal hold overrides ordinary deletion. Account export/deletion requests require identity verification and must preserve records required for public safety, legal compliance or active investigations.

No production data, backups, uploads or credentials may be committed to source control. Cross-border expansion requires a country-specific privacy, retention and lawful-processing review before activation.
