# ECOCLEAN SL Change Log

## v1.0.0-rc.1 — 2026-07-21

- Connected registration, authentication, profiles, reports, assignments, notifications, support conversations, uploads, and audit events to MariaDB-backed APIs.
- Restricted the platform to Citizen, Staff, Supervisor, and Administrator roles.
- Established Sierra Leone-only operational scope with all 16 districts and local councils.
- Added device-camera and gallery evidence for citizen reports and staff completion submissions.
- Enforced supervisor/administrator task assignment, jurisdiction checks, duplicate-task prevention, citizen/staff notifications, and supervisor verification.
- Added branded PDF, native Excel, native Word, CSV, JSON, and GeoJSON report exports.
- Added provider-neutral notification, GIS, backup, monitoring, security, and deployment foundations.
- Hardened proprietary ownership, local infrastructure control, environment handling, and secret exclusions.
