# Pilot Onboarding Guide

Administrators configure jurisdiction, accounts, providers, monitoring and escalation owners. Supervisors verify their municipality scope, assign tasks, review evidence and escalate incidents. Staff sign in on assigned devices, accept only assigned tasks, capture evidence and report connectivity problems. Citizens register, verify profile/contact information, submit location/photo reports, monitor notifications and use the Service Center.

Every pilot participant must complete login, password change, role-denial, report/task or support journey, notification receipt and logout before operational access is approved.
