# National Deployment Readiness

ECOCLEAN owns its application, authentication, database, workflows and GIS records. The schema supports district/municipality/ward/zone scoping and future countries without transferring operational control to a SaaS provider.

National rollout is not yet approved. Required evidence is missing for Sierra Leone authoritative regional/district/municipal/ward polygons, redundant production infrastructure, off-host disaster recovery, real notification delivery, malware scanning, independent security testing, production monitoring retention and multi-region/network load behavior.

National deployment readiness: **78/100**. Expansion to Liberia, Guinea, Gambia, Ghana, Nigeria or wider Africa must use country-versioned boundary imports, local legal/privacy review, country-specific phone/address validation and isolated jurisdiction configuration.
